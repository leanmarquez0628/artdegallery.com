01 — About me

my favorite piece ✎

Hi, I'm Lean Miguel Marquez. For me, drawing is an act of storytelling. What begins as a few quiet pencil lines on paper gradually comes alive as a character, a face, or a narrative.

My creative practice spans traditional illustration. I enjoy creating vibrant anime fan art with markers and colored pencils, as well as executing patient graphite portraits driven by subtle shading. Complementing my character work, I produce ink illustrations, expressive doodles, and architectural design and planning.
